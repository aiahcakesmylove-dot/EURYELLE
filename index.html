Media & Information Literacy

Empowering responsible communication, critical thinking, and ethical use of media in the Information Age

Lesson 1: Introduction to Media and Information Literacy

In the present time, technological advancement is inevitable, and access to information is widely available through technology. Media and Information Literacy emphasizes an individual’s ability to exercise fundamental human rights—especially the right to express opinions using various media platforms and to access information responsibly.

A. Communication and Media Information

Communication is heavily influenced by media and information due to technological advancement. Platforms such as Facebook, Messenger, Instagram, and Twitter allow fast and easy communication.

However, over-reliance on social media can strain personal relationships and expose people to fake news and hoaxes. That is why the principle “Think before you click” is essential.

B. Media, Information, and Technology Literacy

📺 Media Literacy

The ability to understand how media works and determine the appropriate and valuable use of media platforms.

📚 Information Literacy

The skill of acquiring, evaluating, and using information ethically while avoiding plagiarism and copyright violations.

💻 Technology Literacy

The ability to use technology efficiently and independently to access knowledge and communicate information.

Together, these literacies help individuals become responsible users and producers of media and information.

C. Responsible Use of Media and Information

The 21st Century is known as the Information Age. People depend on technology for communication, education, entertainment, and work. Media platforms are also used for advocacy, business, and social awareness.

While media can be an agent of change, misinformation, privacy invasion, and online conflicts remain serious risks. Therefore, users—especially influencers—must ensure that the information they share is accurate and reliable.

Tips for Responsible Media Use

  • Ensure personal information safety – Protect your private data and read terms of use.
  • Get permission – Ask approval before posting photos or videos of others.
  • Think before you click – Share only accurate, helpful, and kind information.
  • Keep your password safe – Update passwords regularly and avoid sharing them.
  • Spread positivity – Promote uplifting and respectful content online.
